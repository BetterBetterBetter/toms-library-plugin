# Screencast 02 — Add episodes to a series

**Goal:** Show how to add a new episode to an ongoing series (member calls,
live events) so it appears in the app.

**Length:** about 1–1½ minutes.

**Recording:** `02-add-series-episodes.mp4`. Shot numbers match the `r.cue(n)`
marks in `test/e2e/specs/screencast-record.spec.ts`; the narrated cut is built
from the **Narration** lines below.

**Have ready:**
- An existing demo series (the recorder uses "Demo — Screencast Member Calls").
- A short demo video link for the episode.

## Shot list

1. **On screen:** **Library → Series**, open the demo series.
   **Narration:** "A series is an ongoing run of episodes, like member calls
   or live events. Go to Library, then Series, and open the series."

2. **On screen:** The **Series settings** box.
   **Narration:** "Series settings controls what each episode is called, and
   whether members see the newest or the oldest episode first."

3. **On screen:** The **Series episodes** box; click its add link.
   **Narration:** "New episodes start from the Series episodes box. The add
   link opens a new episode that already belongs to this series."

4. **On screen:** Type the episode title; paste the video link into the
   **Media** box.
   **Narration:** "Give the episode a title, then paste its video link into
   the Media box, just like a course lesson."

5. **On screen:** The **Library placement** box, then the **Library access**
   box.
   **Narration:** "Placement shows the series it belongs to. You don't set
   access on each episode. Episodes follow the series."

6. **On screen:** Click **Publish**, then return to the series and show the
   episode in **Series episodes**.
   **Narration:** "Publish, and the episode is listed on the series, ready to
   flow to the app."

## Call out

> A series is protected once, on the series itself. You do **not** set access
> on each episode — they inherit the series' access.
