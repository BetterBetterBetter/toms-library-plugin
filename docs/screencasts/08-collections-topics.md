# Screencast 08 — Organise with Collections and Topics

**Goal:** Explain Collections (shelves) versus Topics (cross-cutting tags), and
how to assign them on a course.

**Length:** about 40 seconds.

**Recording:** `08-collections-topics.mp4`. Shot numbers match the `r.cue(n)`
marks in the recorder spec.

**Have ready:**
- At least one Collection and one Topic, plus a demo course.

## Shot list

1. **On screen:** **Library → Collections**.
   **Narration:** "Collections are the shelves content sits on, like
   Masterclasses. Manage them here."

2. **On screen:** **Library → Topics**.
   **Narration:** "Topics are tags that cut across shelves, so members can
   browse by subject."

3. **On screen:** Open the demo course; tick a Collection.
   **Narration:** "On the course itself, tick its collection."

4. **On screen:** Add a Topic in **Library Topics**.
   **Narration:** "Then add its topics."

5. **On screen:** Click **Update**; show the confirmation.
   **Narration:** "Update, and browsing in the app follows. Watch the access
   box too. A collection can bring access with it."

## Call out

> Collection = where it lives (one shelf). Topic = what it's about (many tags).
> Putting a course in a collection that an Access Group covers (e.g.
> Masterclasses) can protect it — check the **Library access** box after
> saving.
