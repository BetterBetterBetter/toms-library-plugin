# Screencast 09 — Schedule or unpublish content

**Goal:** Show how to schedule content to go live later, and how to take live
content down safely without deleting it.

**Length:** about 45 seconds.

**Recording:** `09-schedule-unpublish.mp4`. Shot numbers match the `r.cue(n)`
marks in the recorder spec.

**Have ready:**
- A draft item to schedule, and a published item to unpublish.

## Shot list

1. **On screen:** On a draft item, edit the publish date in the **Publish**
   box to a future date; the button becomes **Schedule**.
   **Narration:** "To release something later, set its publish date in the
   future. The button changes to Schedule."

2. **On screen:** Click **Schedule**; show "Post scheduled for".
   **Narration:** "Schedule it, and it goes live on its own at that time."

3. **On screen:** On a published item, set the status to **Draft** and click
   **Update**.
   **Narration:** "To take something down, switch it to Draft and update. It
   leaves the app, but nothing is lost."

4. **On screen:** Point at **Move to Trash** — don't click it.
   **Narration:** "Avoid the trash unless you're sure. Draft keeps it ready
   to bring back."

## Call out

> Prefer **Draft** over **Trash**. Draft is reversible; deleting is permanent.
